# Research areas

| Research area | Research topics |
| --- | --- |
| Building energy efficiency and environmental control | Air conditioning, natural ventilation, mechanical ventilation, mixed-mode ventilation and energy efficiency |
| Indoor air quality and thermal comfort | CO₂ concentration control, window-opening strategies, air distribution performance and occupant comfort |
| Smart sensing and equipment control | Internet of Things, object detection, data analysis and ventilation equipment control |
| Building information modeling | MEP clash resolution, disease transmission route simulation and visualization |
| Algorithms and resource optimization | Heuristic algorithms, weighting problems and resource allocation for mobile medical screening stations |
| Urban climate and public risk communication | Urban heat islands, temperature risks and news attention to high temperatures |
